## Method

- Data: cTrader **DEMO** (account <DEMO_ACCOUNT_ID>, isLive=False), read-only requests only: GBPUSD M1 bid trendbars 06:00-15:10 UTC plus BID and ASK ticks 06:55-15:05 UTC per day. Raw files are in `/workspace/scratch/lbo_gapfill/raw/`. Every day has 30/30 range bars, and the largest tick gap is 23 s.
- Rules: `hypotheses/LBO.py` v1 unchanged. Range = M1 bid high/low 07:00-07:29 UTC (08:00-08:29 London, BST). OCO stops placed at 07:30:01 UTC: buy at range high + spread, sell at range low - spread, live until 10:30 UTC. Bracket SL 10 pips / TP 20 pips from the fill, time exit 15:00 UTC, 0.01 lot.
- Engine logic copied from `engine.py`/`risk.py`: the tick stream is merged into bid/ask quotes, with a 1-minute clock pass. Buys fill at max(level, ask) + 0.2 pip, sells at min(level, bid) - 0.2 pip. Stop exits are executable + 0.3 pip slippage (and gap fills beyond the stop), stop-first. Targets are limit fills. Commission is $0.06 per round trip. News-window slippage uses the measured curve with the logged calendar. Risk checks (`risk.check_entry`, with normal spread from the engine's `spreads.json`), daily-loss, kill-switch and per-hypothesis kill all apply. Random-direction shadows use the same brackets.
- Equity chain: starts at the engine's $10.85 balance (peak $12.25) after live trade #6, then runs the gap days in order.
- Gap days (from `journal/decisions.jsonl` + `engine_state.json`): Oct 9 (engine down from 01:00 UTC Oct 9 to 10:12 UTC Oct 12), Oct 12 (started 10:12 UTC, after the range was broken), Oct 13-16 (hourly restarts; at the 07:30 UTC order time it had 17-26 range bars and no quote). Oct 8 was traded live, so it is not backfilled. Oct 10/11/17 are weekend days. **Covered: Oct 9, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, each 07:00-15:00 UTC.**
- Simulated as if the engine had been up at 07:30 UTC. On Oct 12 the live engine actually skipped because it started late.
